# Two For The King

**Play in browser:** [https://misaligned-nocturne.github.io/two-for-the-king/](https://misaligned-nocturne.github.io/two-for-the-king/)

A four-course tabletop deduction game of poisoned goblets, courtly tells, and silent regicide.

## How It Works

You are the Royal Taster on the night of a four-course feast. Before each course reaches the Throne, a courtier places **6 goblets** before you (**4 pure, 2 laced with Twin-Numbness**; 7 goblets with 3 poisoned on the final toast).

You have **3 actions** per course to test the tray and read the courtier across from you:

- **Split (`1`)** — Push any 2 goblets across the lacquer tray. The courtier must drink one and return the other. Watch whether their hand stays **Calm**, **Hesitates** over a lethal cup, or **Chokes** when trapped between two poisons.
- **Pin (`2`)** — Test 1 goblet silently under your sleeve with a silver needle (**2 pins** across the entire four-course banquet).
- **Sip (`3`)** — Taste 1 goblet on your own tongue (**1 secret emetic** saves your life once across the entire night).
- **Serve King (`4`)** — Place 2 intact goblets onto the center tray for the King:
  - **2 Pure**: The King drinks unharmed. You survive to the next course (*Loyal path*).
  - **2 Poisoned**: A double-dose of Twin-Numbness numbs every nerve on contact—the King feels nothing until midnight stops his heart (*Treason path*).
  - **1 Pure + 1 Poisoned**: A half-dose fails to numb the throat. The King spits the bitter wine in agony, and the guards take your head on the spot.

Right-click any goblet to mark private notes (`Pure?` / `Poison?`). Press `R` to retry the current course.
