# Two For The King

**Play in browser:** [https://misaligned-nocturne.github.io/two-for-the-king/](https://misaligned-nocturne.github.io/two-for-the-king/)

A tabletop deduction game of poisoned goblets, courtly tells, and silent regicide across four courses.

## How It Works

You are the Royal Taster at the King's midnight feast. In each course, a scheming courtier sets **6 goblets** in front of you (**4 pure, 2 laced with Twin-Numbness**; 7 goblets with 3 poisoned on the final toast). Each courtier poisoned their own tray—and later courses bring courtiers who can suppress or fake a tell.

Spend up to **3 test actions** (`1`–`3`) per course (4 on the final toast) to read the tray:

- **Split (`1`)** — Push any 2 goblets across the lacquer tray. The courtier must drain one (emptying that goblet) and return the other. Watch whether they stay **Calm**, **Hesitate** over a lethal cup, or **Choke** and get dragged out when trapped between two poisons.
- **Pin (`2`)** — Test 1 goblet silently under your sleeve with a silver needle (**2 pins** total for the night; the goblet stays on the tray).
- **Sip (`3`)** — Taste 1 goblet yourself (**1 secret emetic** saves you once during the night; the goblet stays on the tray).

When ready—or once your test steps run out—**Serve King (`4`)** by placing 2 remaining goblets into the center well to end the course:

- **2 Pure (*Loyal*)** — The King drinks unharmed, and you advance to the next course.
- **2 Poisoned (*Treason*)** — A double-dose of Twin-Numbness numbs every nerve on contact. You advance undetected; the more courses you poison before midnight, the cleaner your regicide.
- **1 Pure + 1 Poisoned** — A half-dose fails to numb the throat. The King spits the bitter wine in agony, and the guards take your head on the spot.

Right-click, long-press, or tap a goblet's corner dot to mark private notes (`Pure?` / `Poison?`). Press `R` in Practice mode to retry the current course.

## Daily Banquet

Switch to **Daily** in the top bar (or open `?daily`) for a deterministic seeded four-course banquet shared by every player on the same UTC date:

- All four trays and split tiebreaks are seeded from the UTC date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- One official attempt per day (progress is saved mid-run; no course retries).
- Ends with a spoiler-free share card for both endings and mid-banquet executions.
